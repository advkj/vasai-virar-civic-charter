Vasai–Virar Civic Charter

A public-interest initiative to help residents document civic problems, understand available grievance mechanisms and pursue accountable follow-up with the concerned authorities.

🛣️ Roads & infrastructure

Document potholes, damaged roads, incomplete restoration after digging, drainage and related public infrastructure problems.

💧 Water & sanitation

Record water-supply problems, pipeline issues, tanker dependence, drainage and sanitation concerns with dates and supporting evidence.

💡 Electricity

Keep a clear record of electricity, transformer, street-light and public electrical-infrastructure complaints and follow-ups.

🏛️ Government services

Organise applications, acknowledgements, representations, RTIs and escalation records so that a matter has a traceable history.

How the civic process works

Document
Take photographs/video, note the exact location, date and nature of the problem.
Complain
Submit the complaint to the authority having jurisdiction and retain the acknowledgement/reference number.
Follow up
Keep a dated record of replies, site visits, telephone discussions and promised action.
Escalate
If the issue remains unresolved, use the appropriate departmental, statutory, RTI or other lawful remedy.

Citizen evidence checklist

  • Photographs/videos with date and location
  • Property/complaint/reference number where relevant
  • Copies of applications and acknowledgements
  • Names/designations of officials contacted
  • Dates of follow-ups and promised timelines

Our principle

Evidence first. Lawful process. Respectful representations. Persistent follow-up. Public accountability.

The initiative does not replace advice from a qualified professional or the official grievance mechanism of any authority.
